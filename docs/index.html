daikin-altherma-esp32

Flash the monitoring firmware onto your ESP32 straight from the browser — then set up WiFi, MQTT and your Daikin model from a phone. No toolchain, no cloud.

Your browser can't do Web Serial — use desktop Chrome or Edge. Serial access was blocked — allow it and retry.

The installer auto-detects your chip (esp32 / esp32s3 / esp32c3 / esp32c6) and does a full flash.

After flashing

  1. Join the device's daikin-altherma-esp32-setup WiFi network; the setup page opens automatically (else http://192.168.4.1). Enter your WiFi, save — it reboots.
  2. Open http://daikin-altherma-esp32.local. Under Setup → Heat pump pick your indoor / outdoor / tank model and the RX/TX pins you wired to X10A.
  3. Under Setup → MQTT enter your broker for Home Assistant auto-discovery.
  4. Wire the ESP to the heat pump's X10A port (power off first — see the guide).
