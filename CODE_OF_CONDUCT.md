# Code of Conduct

This is a small hobby project. The short version: **be decent to people, and be precise about
hardware.**

## What is expected

- **Assume good faith.** Everyone here is guessing at an undocumented protocol on a heat pump they
  paid for. Wrong guesses are how this codebase got correct.
- **Be specific, not dismissive.** "That decode is wrong" is not useful; "on an ERGA04E, register
  0x60 offset 6 reads 231.6 °C, which is impossible" is.
- **Say what you actually verified.** Especially about hardware. "Built and tested on my unit",
  "compiles only, no board", and "reasoned about it" are three different claims, and conflating them
  is how a wrong value ships.
- **Accept that some things get declined**, and that a maintained hobby project moves at hobby speed.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning or discriminatory comments, sexualised language or imagery,
sustained disruption, deliberate intimidation, publishing others' private information, and
persistently pushing a change after a clear decision. This applies in issues, pull requests, commit
messages, code comments and any other project space.

Also not acceptable, because of what this project touches: **encouraging anyone to do something
unsafe with mains voltage or a refrigerant circuit.** Wiring instructions in this repo say "breaker
OFF" for a reason. Don't post shortcuts around that.

## Scope

Anywhere this project is represented — repository spaces above, and public spaces where someone is
acting on the project's behalf.

## Reporting

Report a concern **privately to the maintainer** ([@0Bu](https://github.com/0Bu)) rather than in a
public thread. Reports are handled confidentially, and the reporter's identity is not disclosed to the
person reported.

There is one maintainer, so there is no appeals committee and no guaranteed response time. If the
concern is *about* the maintainer and that makes a direct report untenable, GitHub's own
[abuse reporting](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) is the escalation path.

## Consequences

At the maintainer's discretion and in proportion to the conduct: a private word, editing or removing
the offending content, locking a thread, or blocking the account from the repository. Anything
involving threats or targeted harassment goes straight to the last option.
